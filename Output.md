Sample Output – Examination Seating System Using BST

1. Valid Input

Enter number of students: 5
Enter preorder sequence of roll numbers: 12 10 9 11 13

2. Output

Inorder Traversal: 9 10 11 12 13
Level-order Traversal: 12 10 13 9 11

3. Invalid Input Example

Enter number of students: 3
Enter preorder sequence of roll numbers: 10 15 5
Invalid BST preorder sequence.

Conclusion

The program constructs a Binary Search Tree from a preorder sequence and displays its inorder and level-order traversals.
